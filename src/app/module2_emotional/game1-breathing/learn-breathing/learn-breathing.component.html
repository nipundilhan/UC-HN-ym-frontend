Welcome to the Whispers of the Sphinx, brave adventurer!

As exams approach, stress can cloud even the sharpest of minds. But fear not! Here, you'll discover breathing techniques that help to calm your nerves, sharpen your focus, and boost your confidence.

In this quest, you’ll learn four breathing techniques to help you along the way:

Box Breathing

4-7-8 Breathing

Belly Breathing

Alternate Nostril Breathing

How to Get Started

  • Find a Quiet Place: Choose a comfortable and quiet spot where you won't be disturbed.
  • Sit Comfortably: Sit in a relaxed posture, either on a chair or on the floor. Keep your back straight but not stiff.
  • Practice Regularly: Aim for a few minutes each day to make breathing practices a part of your routine. Use the techniques taught in the application, such as Box breathing and 4-7-8 breathing, to start. For belly breathing and alternate nostril breathing, practice manually and log your progress to track your journey.

How to Get Practice and Log Breathing Sessions

Within this application, you can choose to either practice or log breathing sessions based on your needs. However, among the breathing techniques available, only the Box Breathing and 4-7-8 Breathing techniques can be practiced within the app with helpful animations. Other techniques, such as Belly Breathing and Alternate Nostril Breathing, need to be practiced manually on your own, but you can still log the details of these sessions within the app.

Here's how to log and practice breathing techniques:

  • Practice Session (Only applicable for Box Breathing and 4-7-8 Breathing): To practice these techniques within the app, click on Practice Session. Then select the breathing technique (Box or 4-7-8) from the dropdown menu and click Next. Choose the number of cycles you want to practice. Optionally, you can enable relaxation music by ticking the checkbox (you can unmute it at any time). Your session details will be recorded automatically.
  • Log session: You can log details of any breathing session you practice on your own (for any technique taught within the app), click on Log Session. Then select the breathing technique from the dropdown menu and click Next. Enter the duration of your practice (in minutes). Click Submit to save the details.
How to Get Practice and Log Breathing Sessions

Earn Points and Unlock Badges!

Each breathing session you log will earn you points to show your progress. The more consistent you are, the more points you’ll collect.

Here are the badges you can unlock:

  • Beginner Badge: Whispers of the Sphinx Earned after logging your first session. Congratulations on taking the first step toward calm!
  • Master Badge: Whispers of the Sphinx Unlocked after consistently logging your fifth session. You're building a healthy habit—keep going!

Ready to Begin?

Explore the "Learn" section to master these breathing techniques. When you're ready, head to the "Play" section to start practicing or log your manual sessions.

With every session, you'll feel calmer and more focused for your exams. Unlock badges, earn points, and make stress a thing of the past. Your journey starts now!
