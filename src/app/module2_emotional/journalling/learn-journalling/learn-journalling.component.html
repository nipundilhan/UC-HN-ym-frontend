Welcome to the Calm of the Oasis : Journalling Mastery, brave adventurer!

As you embark on this journey, I'll share powerful tips to help you master the practice of journaling. Together, we'll turn your thoughts and reflections into treasures that fuel your confidence and focus during exams. Click next, and let's uncover the secrets to starting your journaling quest!

In this quest, you’ll learn three powerful journaling techniques to help you along the way:

Gratitude Journaling

Self-Compassion Journaling

Reflective Journaling

These techniques will guide you on your journey to mastering your thoughts and building your confidence.

How to get started?

  • Find Your Moment: Choose a quiet time in your day to journal, whether it’s in the morning, after studying, or before bed.
  • Use Prompts: Journalling prompts can help guide your thoughts. (Hint: The game will provide you with some!)
  • Start Small: A few sentences or bullet points are enough—there’s no pressure to write a lot.

How to get started?

  • Be Honest: Write freely and without judgment. This is your space to be yourself.
  • Stay Consistent: Try to journal regularly, even if it’s just a few times a week.

With these tips in mind, you’re ready to begin your journaling adventure!

How to play the game?

Select Your Journaling Technique: Choose from the techniques you’ve learned.

Write Your Journal Entries: Select two prompts from the dropdown and write your thoughts. You can even add your own personal prompt to make it unique!

Your Privacy Matters: Rest assured, your journal entries are encrypted and stored safely. Only you can view your entries—your thoughts are private and secure.

How to play the game?

Earn Points and Unlock Badges!

Each time you log a journal entry, you earn valuable points that help you level up in the game. The more you write, the more points you collect!

As you journal, you’ll also unlock badges and achievements! Here’s what you’ll get:

  • Beginner Badge: Calm of the Scarab Awarded after logging your second journal entry—this means you’ve officially started your journaling journey. Great job!
  • Master Badge: Calm of the Scarab Awarded after logging your fifth journal entry—you’re on your way to building a powerful journaling habit. Keep it up!
